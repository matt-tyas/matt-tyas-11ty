I lead design.
I do design.

Twelve years at Co-op, four as Head of Design. Twenty years across enterprise, agency, higher education and co-founder of Manchester's Finest.

About

I lead design across the full spectrum of the craft: service design, user research, content design, interaction and product design. I have stayed hands on throughout. I still prototype, still write code, I still observe users — I am an enquisitive person who likes to make things.

Most of what I am proud of took determination. The Co-op Experience Library started with no mandate and no budget and is now the foundation of the platform the organisation builds on.

I co-founded Manchester's Finest. The biggest most trusted platform that exists to support the best independents the city has to offer. It is a live, profitable, commercial product with real profit and loss, real investment decisions, and an audience that tells us immediately when we get it wrong. It has taught me more about experience as a driver of value than theory ever has.

I build and scale people-first, high-performing, psychologically safe teams, creating experiences that drive commercial and user-centred outcomes.

I believe in:

  • outcome based visions that align to customer and business needs that empower teams to solve problems, not just deliver
  • using researched based design to achieve user and business goals
  • prototyping in code and showing users early and often
  • the power of systems, technology and efficiency to empower teams to innovate and make better decisions
  • collaboration and communication to create the best outcomes
